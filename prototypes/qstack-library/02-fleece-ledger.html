Direction notes
  1. Letterpress card construction. Gallery cards take a 3:4 stationery aspect with a debossed double frame, wide margins, a collector set mark, and the star count as a large serif numeral. Serves locality: every control and every identity mark lives on the card face itself, nothing a click away.
  2. The fleece edge, exactly two states. Base cards carry the 1px line border. Earned cards, 25 stars or in rotation, carry a 2px matte gold foil edge built only from the two gold tokens. Serves gold scarcity and two-states-not-tiers: no rarity names, no ladder, gold spent only where the team earned it.
  3. Crest watermark in ink. The ram crest sits on each gallery card at 5 percent ink opacity, never gold, so the foil edge stays the only gold on the face and body text stays legible. Serves gold scarcity and the AA floor.
  4. The earned shine. A single-hue gold sweep plays once on first sight, on keyboard focus, and right after starring; pointer hover only adds tracking and up to 3 degrees of tilt. Serves input parity, keyboard gets the same ceremony as pointer, and it all stands down under reduced motion.
  5. The gallery as a collection. Shelf rows grouped by role family with quiet counts and hairline shelf rules; starring answers at the star with a ring pulse and a card-local undo; moves flash on the card they changed. Serves locality-first feedback: the state change is visible exactly where it happened.
